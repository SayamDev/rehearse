Scope: whole Rehearse app (landing, setup, practice and notes, summary, archive, Remember, recall drill, Me). Mode: Operate. Replaces the Rehearsal Room world; product truth, features and data are unchanged.
Audience and job: teens and adults, many first-time or vulnerable job seekers, practising alone on a phone. User brief: unique, fun, playful, inviting, trustworthy, game-like, friendly, arty.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Every skill you practise becomes a bold die-cut sticker you earn; progress is a sticker sheet filling up. Refuses the green-mascot learning-app default and the dark productivity-tool default.
OWN-WORLD: Sticker sheet on a cool lilac-white backing (#f3f4fb; dark: deep ink-violet #17162a). Four flat sticker inks: tomato #ff5a3c (go and record), sky #3ba7ff (takes and info), lime #b8e62e (earned and improved), sun #ffc83d (XP, level, streak). Every sticker has a thick white die-cut border and a short soft shadow, and sits at a slight tilt. Text on inks is ink #1c1b2e. Panels are white sheets with 20px corners. Bricolage Grotesque for display and numbers, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for reading text. Icons are Phosphor fill inside round die-cut stickers.
STORY: Name a job, answer a question, get notes, retake it, and watch a new sticker slap onto the page when you improve. Save answers and practise them from memory.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Landing: big chunky question headline left-anchored with a tilted sticker ("free, no signup") on its corner, the job input and a tomato sticker button beside it, then the sample notes card with a Take 2 sticker and a lime +1.8 sticker. Practice: question large, round tomato record sticker centre.
FORM: The Sticker Book, candidate 3 of 7 (reroll 1), seed key 6f566f5c. Raises: a folded flag sticker marks where you stopped; state is shown with marks as well as colour (dashed outline for not yet earned).
SIGNATURE: The sticker slap. On a retake the new score sticker drops in from slightly larger and tilted and settles with a spring, and the improvement sticker lands beside it. Skill stickers on the Me sheet slap in when earned.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
